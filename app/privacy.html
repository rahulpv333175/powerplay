Powerplay

Privacy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Powerplay is an unofficial fan game. This page says what we store about you, why, and how to have it removed. We keep as little as we can.

What we store

  • A guest account. When you open Multiplayer we create an account with a random ID and no personal details. It is remembered in your browser.
  • Your display name and team names, as you type them. Other players in your rooms can see these.
  • Your game records: the rooms you played, the players you picked, results, and your single-player trophy cabinet.
  • Your email address, only if you choose to sign in with Google or an email link. If you use Google we receive your email address and basic profile (name and picture) from Google. We never see your Google password.

We do not ask for your phone number, address, date of birth, contacts or location, and we do not collect payment details. The game has no advertising and no advertising trackers.

What we use it for

  • To sign you in and keep your profile, so it is there on your next visit and on other devices.
  • To run multiplayer rooms and show records between you and the people you play with.
  • To keep the service working and stop abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing email. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Stored in your browser

The game keeps your guest account, your draft or season in progress and your room details in your browser's local storage, so a refresh does not lose them. Clearing your browser data removes them. We do not use advertising cookies.

Who else handles your data

We use a small number of services to run the game. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Supabase stores accounts and game records, and handles sign-in.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Netlify and Render host the website and the game server. Like any web host, they process your IP address and basic request details to deliver the pages.

These services may store data on servers outside India.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and records until you ask us to delete them. Guest accounts that are never linked to Google or an email, and their records, may be removed after a long period without use.

Your choices

  • You can play single-player without creating any account.
  • You can sign out at any time from the Account button.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account and records. Email us from the address on the account and we will act on it within 30 days.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us an email address, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top.
